Air Domination

Air Domination is an exciting strategy board game where players take on the role of elite pilots tasked with completing high-stakes military missions to earn medals and secure victory. Each player chooses one of four advanced 5th-generation jet fighters, the F-22 Raptor, Dassault Rafale, J-20 Mighty Dragon, or Sukhoi Su-57 Felon. Players embark on missions, engaging in both Player versus Player (PvP) and Player versus Environment (PvE) challenges, with the option to play solo or in teams. The ultimate goal is to outmaneuver your opponents, complete missions, and collect medals to prove your dominance in the skies.
