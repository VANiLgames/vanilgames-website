Game components

This exciting board game features a dynamic map made up of 9 interlocking boards, providing a versatile battlefield for each session. Players can choose from 4 aircraft carrier boards and 4 jet fighter boards to strategize and deploy their forces. With 33 thrilling missions to complete, the game also includes metallic jet fighter figures, 3 specialized dice for attack, defence, and movement, and 9 medals to award players for their victories. The game is supported by clear rules in both Czech and English. It also contains 84 detailed tokens representing jet fighters, weapons, defensive units and weapon factories, adding depth to every battle. Get ready to dive into an immersive aerial combat experience!
